# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-07-26)

### Initial Release

- Forecast entry management (planned expenses, actual expenses, credits, account payments)
- Six status levels (considering, estimated, reserved, committed, incurred, cancelled)
- Three forecast scenarios (Actual, Committed, Full Forecast)
- Trip budget and remaining budget calculations
- Category budgets with progress tracking and over-budget warnings
- Payment account tracking with outstanding and projected balances
- Multi-currency support with frozen exchange rates
- Native TREK cost integration (read, create, link/unlink)
- Planned-to-actual conversion with variance reporting
- CSV export with formula injection prevention
- Full audit history for all entry changes
- Responsive UI with light/dark mode support
- 37 unit tests covering money arithmetic, forecast calculations, and account balances
